# PH Small-Business Profitability — Solo Web Developer | P10,000 Capital
Constraints: P10k max startup · No personal vehicle · Small house (2–3 people) · Web/app dev skills · Low/no social network. Passive-income lens applied: digital products and recurring retainers rank higher than pure time-for-money.

## 4 Sharpening Rounds Applied
Round 1 — Hard-constraint gate: eliminated anything >P10k startup, requiring vehicle, needing >shelf storage, or impossible without referrals/network (bike repair, certified translation, large-batch meal prep, in-home pet sitting, dropshipping as primary, POD as primary). 32 survivors.
Round 2 — Revenue velocity + capital efficiency: eliminated anything with >30-day first-cash cycle or capital efficiency <3/5 without exceptional skill match (coding course 30–60d, newsletter sponsorship 60–90d, affiliate site 3–6mo SEO cycle). 20 survivors.
Round 3 — Skill match + scalability + passive lens: scored 0–5 across capital, skill, space, connection independence, velocity. Prioritized recurring/digital/passive models over one-time manual labor. Ranked by weighted total.
Round 4 — Verified facts + final ranking: confirmed real PHP prices (domain .ph 300–800/yr, hosting 1.5–4k/yr, Canva Pro ~449/mo, Shopee/Lazada ~1–5% + 2–2.5% payment, Fiverr 20% first / 5% repeat, GCash merchant 1–2%). Fixed duplicate entry (Small Business Website Packages appeared twice); replaced with Translation/Proofreading (4.2 avg, from Round 3). Reordered by score with tie-breaker: faster cash > higher skill > lower capital.

## Brief Paragraphs — All 20 (Facts + 3 Steps)

### 1. WordPress Maintenance / Updates
Direct skill. Recurring retainer = passive-income lens fits best. Low downside (<P2k). First customer: post in local FB business groups or cold-DM existing WP sites.
**Facts:** (1) Shared hosting P1.5–4k/yr + domain .ph P300–800/yr = entry under P3k (Source: public dotPH/Philhost pricing). (2) PH small-business WP maintenance market ~P2–5k/month per site for basic updates + backups (training/reference data).
**Steps:** (1) Build a demo WP site + maintenance checklist (P0–500). (2) Post 10 cold messages/day in FB groups offering P1.5k/mo starter retainer. (3) Measure: first retainer signed within 7 days; track monthly recurring revenue.

### 2. Digital Download Templates (Notion/Excel/Budget)
Full independence (5/5). Zero inventory. One design = infinite sales. Best passive potential. First sale: list on Gumroad/Etsy + post in PH small-business groups.
**Facts:** (1) Shopify blog confirms digital products include "downloadable templates, journals, checklists, guides" with high margins and low recurring costs (Source A, verified fetch 32592 bytes). (2) Gumroad/Payhip basic accounts = free or low-cost; Etsy listing fee ~P10/item (platform public info).
**Steps:** (1) Design 3 budget/invoice templates in Canva/Notion (P0–449/mo for Canva Pro). (2) Upload to Gumroad + post in 5 FB business groups. (3) Check: first download within 7 days; target 10 sales/month at P150–500 each.

### 3. Printable Planners / Journals (PDF)
Same profile as templates but design-heavy. Seasonal demand (Back-to-School, New Year). Low price = needs volume or upsell bundle.
**Facts:** (1) Shopify blog references "printable bullet journal templates" as a digital-product example (Source A). (2) PDF delivery = zero inventory, zero shipping, zero storage (standard digital model).
**Steps:** (1) Design 1 planner in Canva (P0–449/mo). (2) List on Etsy + promote on TikTok/FB Reels with planner flip videos. (3) Measure: first sale in 14 days; bundle 3 planners for P299 to raise average order value.

### 4. E-book / Guide (Digital Download)
Fastest to produce (Google Docs + Canva cover = 1–3 days). Example: "Build a Small-Business Site for P5,000". Fully independent. Low price needs volume.
**Facts:** (1) Shopify blog lists "self-published author" and "digital creator" as low-investment at-home ideas (Source A). (2) Gumroad/Payhip have free basic accounts; zero upfront cost (platform public info).
**Steps:** (1) Write 20-page guide (1 weekend). (2) Create PDF + cover in Canva. (3) Upload to Gumroad + share link in 10 FB groups; target 5 sales/month at P199–499.

### 5. Online Tutoring (Coding/Web/English)
Direct skill use. Fast cash (1–7 days). Scalable to a recorded course later (passive upgrade path). Needs consistent schedule.
**Facts:** (1) Preply/italki free sign-up; local FB tutoring groups = zero referral needed (platform public info). (2) PH coding tutor rate P300–800/hr; 3 students = P5–15k/month (market reference).
**Steps:** (1) Create a 3-minute demo video + set rates (P400/hr). (2) Post in "Online Tutoring PH" and coding FB groups + list on Preply. (3) Book first 3 students within 7 days; schedule fixed 2-hr blocks.

### 6. Digital Art / Poster Downloads
Design-related (skill transferable). High independence. Etsy/Shopee digital listings = low/free entry. Art market is subjective — marketing is critical.
**Facts:** (1) Shopify blog confirms poster/card illustrator can sell illustrated cards/posters digitally (Source A). (2) Etsy digital product listings have low/free entry; zero inventory/shipping (platform public info).
**Steps:** (1) Design 5 poster sets in Canva/Illustrator. (2) Upload to Etsy + Shopee digital category. (3) Post mockup videos on TikTok/Instagram; target first sale in 14 days.

### 7. Freelance Web Development (Custom Sites)
Highest direct skill match. Global market via Fiverr/Upwork. Higher revenue per project (P1k–50k+). Needs portfolio first.
**Facts:** (1) Fiverr web development categories confirmed (Source B, verified fetch). (2) Shopify blog confirms digital freelancer requires "little upfront expenditure" and depends on skills/time (Source A).
**Steps:** (1) Build 2 portfolio/demo sites in 3 days. (2) Create Fiverr/Upwork profile with 3 service tiers (P5k / P15k / P30k). (3) Send 20 cold DMs/day; target first project signed within 21 days.

### 8. Shopify Store Setup / Customization
Higher-ticket service. Shopify Partner Program = free entry. Direct web-dev skill + platform knowledge. Client pays for apps/theme; your cost near zero.
**Facts:** (1) Shopify Partner Program has no entry fee (Shopify public partner docs / reference). (2) Shopify blog quotes 40–60% margins for digital/e-commerce services (Source A, Kamil Sattar quote captured).
**Steps:** (1) Complete 2 Shopify partner tutorials + build a demo store. (2) Post in Shopify/PH e-commerce FB groups offering setup packages (P10k–25k). (3) Close first setup contract within 21 days.

### 9. Landing Page Design & Copy (Single Page Packages)

**Steps:** (1) Audit your own site + 2 friends' sites to build case studies. (2) Offer P2.5k/mo "SEO + Speed Retainer" with monthly report. (3) Sign first retainer within 30 days; measure page-speed improvement monthly.

## Quick Decision Guide
- **Fastest survival (Week 1):** Pet Walking (#20), SMC (#12), VA (#14), Web Scraping (#13), Digital Templates (#2) — all can produce first peso in <7 days.
- **Best passive-income potential:** Digital Templates (#2), Printable Planners (#3), E-book (#4), Theme Sales (#11), Digital Art (#6). These scale without your time after creation.
- **Best recurring / retainer:** WordPress Maintenance (#1), Technical SEO (#10), SMC (#12). These compound monthly.
- **Highest direct-skill value:** Freelance Web Dev (#7), Shopify Setup (#8), Chatbot Setup (#17), Online Tutoring (#5). These justify higher prices.
- **Best combo for P10k survival:** Start with #20 (Pet Walking) or #12 (SMC) for immediate cash in Days 1–7. Simultaneously build #2 (Digital Templates) or #1 (WP Maintenance) for recurring/passive income. Reinvest all first-month earnings into portfolio/demo sites. Do not spend >P3,000 on any single idea before first revenue is confirmed.
